Shih Tzu Boarding in Barrackpore: Gentle Care for Your Little Companion

Published: April 2026 | By Emily's Pet Heaven Team | Pet Boarding Barrackpore

Shih Tzus are one of the most beloved small breed dogs in Barrackpore, known for their affectionate personality, beautiful coats and gentle temperament. These royal companions need boarding facilities that understand small breed care, coat maintenance and the specific needs of toy breeds. At Emily's Pet Heaven, we provide gentle, attentive care that makes Shih Tzus feel safe and pampered during their stay.

Why Shih Tzus Need Specialised Small Breed Boarding

Shih Tzus have very different needs compared to large breed dogs. They are sensitive to temperature extremes, require daily coat maintenance, and can become stressed if housed near large, energetic dogs. A boarding facility that treats all dogs the same is not ideal for Shih Tzus.

At Emily's Pet Heaven, our small breed boarding includes:

  • Separate small breed boarding area away from large dogs
  • Climate-controlled rooms (crucial during Kolkata's hot summers)
  • Daily coat brushing to prevent matting
  • Gentle, patient handling by trained staff
  • Small-portion, scheduled feeding with premium small breed food
  • Low-stress socialisation with other small dogs

Shih Tzu Coat Care During Boarding

The Shih Tzu's beautiful double coat requires daily attention, especially during extended boarding stays. In Barrackpore's humid climate, Shih Tzu coats are prone to matting, tangling and skin issues if not properly maintained.

Our grooming team provides daily brushing for boarding Shih Tzus, checking for tangles, mats and skin irritation. For dogs on extended stays, we offer full grooming sessions including bathing, coat trimming, nail clipping and ear cleaning.

We use gentle, hypoallergenic grooming products suitable for Shih Tzu's sensitive skin. If your Shih Tzu has a specific grooming routine or preferred products, please let us know and we will follow your instructions.

Diet and Feeding for Boarding Shih Tzus

Shih Tzus are small dogs with specific nutritional needs. They are prone to obesity if overfed and can have dental issues that make certain food types difficult. During boarding, we follow precise feeding schedules:

  • 2-3 small measured meals per day (based on age and weight)
  • Premium small breed kibble or wet food as per your preference
  • Fresh water available at all times
  • Dental-friendly treats as rewards

We accommodate home-cooked diets, specialty foods and medication schedules without any issues.

Exercise and Socialisation for Shih Tzus

Shih Tzus don't need the intense exercise that Labradors or German Shepherds require, but they do benefit from gentle daily activity and social interaction. Our Shih Tzu exercise programme includes:

  • Short walks (15-20 minutes) during cool parts of the day
  • Indoor play sessions with interactive toys
  • Gentle socialisation with other small, calm dogs
  • Lap time and cuddles with our caregivers

We avoid outdoor exercise during peak heat hours (11 AM - 4 PM in summer) as Shih Tzus are brachycephalic (short-nosed) and prone to overheating.
