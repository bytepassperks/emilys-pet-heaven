Our Story

A Journey Born From Love For Animals

Emily's Pet Heaven was founded in 2025 by Emily, a lifelong animal lover who dreamed of creating a sanctuary where pets could receive the same love and care they get at home. What started as a small boarding facility has grown into a comprehensive pet care center serving thousands of happy pets and their families.

Our journey began with a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated like family. This philosophy guides everything we do, from the way we design our facilities to how we train our staff. Today, we're proud to be one of the most trusted pet care centers in the region.
