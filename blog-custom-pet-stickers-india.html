Custom Viral Pet Stickers in India: Your Pet Starring in This Month's Memes

Published: June 2026 | By Emily's Pet Heaven Team | Custom Viral Pet Stickers

Meme culture moves fast in India — one week it's a train-riding golden retriever, the next it's a Bengali reel audio everyone in Kolkata is lip-syncing. What if your own dog or cat could star in those exact memes? That's the idea behind Custom Viral Pet Stickers by Emily's Pet Heaven: you upload your pet's photo, and we turn them into 12 big die-cut stickers based on memes trending this month and last month — including Indian and Bengali virals.

What are custom viral pet stickers?

Custom viral pet stickers are personalised die-cut stickers where YOUR pet — with their real face, fur colours and markings kept recognizable — is illustrated into current viral meme scenarios. Unlike generic meme sticker packs sold across India (which recycle memes that are years old), each pack is generated fresh at order time from what's actually trending right now.

Each order includes:

  • 12 big stickers across 2 premium A4 sticker sheets (6 large stickers per sheet)
  • Memes from this month + last month — Indian trends, Bengali/Kolkata virals, global hits
  • Die-cut style with dashed cut outlines so you can cut each sticker easily
  • A surprise mix — you know your pet stars in it, but not which memes you'll get!

How does ordering work?

Ordering takes under two minutes on the order page:

  • Upload 1–2 clear photos of your pet and enter their name (some memes use it!)
  • Enter your delivery PIN code — you instantly see the exact courier charge
  • Confirm on WhatsApp — pack price ₹499 + one shipping charge for both sheets
  • We create, print and ship with tracking anywhere in India

Why fresh memes matter

Search Amazon or Meesho for "meme stickers India" and you'll find the same recycled packs — memes from 2019 sold in 2026. Trends have a shelf life of weeks. Because our sticker packs are generated per order from live trend feeds, a pack ordered in July features July's virals; a pack ordered for Durga Puja features whatever Bengal is laughing at that week. No two months — and no two pets — produce the same pack.

Perfect gift for pet parents

Pet parents already put their dogs and cats on mugs and t-shirts — stickers are cheaper, more shareable and endlessly useful: laptops, bottles, scooters, journals, phone cases. A surprise pack starring someone's own pet in this month's memes is the kind of gift that gets photographed and posted immediately. It works for birthdays, adoption anniversaries ("gotcha days"), or just because your friend won't stop showing you photos of their indie.
