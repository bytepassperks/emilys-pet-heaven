Labrador Boarding in Barrackpore: The Complete Guide for Lab Owners

Published: April 2026 | By Emily's Pet Heaven Team | Pet Boarding Barrackpore

Labradors are one of the most popular dog breeds in Barrackpore and across India, known for their friendly temperament, boundless energy and loyal nature. If you are a Labrador owner planning a trip or looking for reliable daycare, finding the right boarding facility is essential to keep your Lab happy and healthy. At Emily's Pet Heaven in Barrackpore, we have extensive experience caring for Labradors and understand their unique needs.

Why Labradors Need Specialised Boarding Care

Labradors are high-energy dogs that require structured daily activity, proper nutrition and social interaction. A boarding facility that simply offers a kennel and two meals a day is not sufficient for a Labrador. Labs thrive on exercise, mental stimulation and human interaction — without these, they can become anxious, destructive or depressed during boarding.

At Emily's Pet Heaven, we design customised boarding plans for each Labrador that include:

  • 2-3 structured outdoor exercise sessions daily (morning, afternoon, evening)
  • Supervised socialisation with compatible dogs
  • Interactive toys and puzzle feeders for mental stimulation
  • Swimming or water play opportunities (Labs love water!)
  • Nutritious, protein-rich meals appropriate for active breeds

Labrador Diet During Boarding

Labradors are notorious for their big appetites, and improper feeding can quickly lead to weight gain. During boarding at Emily's Pet Heaven, we follow precise feeding schedules based on your Lab's age, weight and activity level.

For adult Labradors, we typically serve two measured meals per day with premium dog food. We accommodate specific dietary requirements including grain-free diets, raw feeding plans or veterinary-prescribed meals. If your Lab is on medication, our team administers it precisely on schedule.

We monitor food intake and report any changes in appetite, which can be an early indicator of stress or health issues. For Labrador puppies, we follow more frequent feeding schedules (3-4 times daily) with puppy-specific nutrition.

Exercise Requirements for Labradors in Boarding

A Labrador that does not get enough exercise becomes a Labrador that chews furniture, digs holes and barks excessively. Our facility in Barrackpore includes outdoor play areas where Labs can run, fetch and play freely under supervision.

Our daily exercise programme for Labradors includes:

  • Morning session (30-45 min): Brisk walk or jog, followed by fetch games
  • Midday socialisation (20-30 min): Supervised group play with compatible dogs
  • Evening wind-down (20-30 min): Gentle walk and calm interaction time

During the hot Kolkata summers, we adjust exercise timing to early morning and late evening to prevent heat exhaustion, and provide constant access to fresh water throughout the day.

Common Labrador Health Concerns During Boarding

Responsible boarding means watching for breed-specific health issues. Labradors are prone to hip dysplasia, ear infections (especially in the humid Kolkata climate), obesity and skin allergies. Our trained staff monitors for signs of discomfort including limping, ear scratching, excessive panting or changes in eating behaviour.

We check ears daily during the monsoon season when humidity increases infection risk. If any health concern arises, our 24/7 veterinary coordination ensures your Lab receives prompt medical attention.

Preparing Your Labrador for Boarding

To ensure a smooth boarding experience for your Labrador at Emily's Pet Heaven, we recommend:

  • Bring your Lab's favourite toy or blanket for comfort
  • Provide feeding instructions and any dietary supplements
  • Share vaccination records (rabies, distemper, parvovirus must be current)
  • Inform us of any behavioural quirks, fears or allergies
  • If it is your Lab's first boarding experience, consider a trial daycare visit first

We offer doorstep pickup from across Barrackpore, Barasat, Madhyamgram and surrounding areas, so you don't need to worry about transporting your Lab yourself.
