Persian Cat Boarding in Barrackpore: Safe, Quiet and Expert Feline Care

Published: April 2026 | By Emily's Pet Heaven Team | Pet Boarding Barrackpore

Persian cats are one of the most popular and beloved feline breeds in Barrackpore and across India, known for their luxurious coats, calm disposition and affectionate personality. Boarding a Persian cat requires a facility that understands feline needs — especially the specific grooming, dietary and environmental requirements of this breed. At Emily's Pet Heaven, we provide dedicated cat boarding with quiet, separate spaces designed specifically for feline comfort.

Why Persian Cats Need Dedicated Boarding

Persian cats are inherently more sensitive to environmental changes than many other breeds. They stress easily, require daily coat maintenance and are prone to respiratory issues due to their flat facial structure. A boarding facility that houses cats alongside loud dogs is not suitable for Persian cats.

Emily's Pet Heaven provides:

  • Completely separate cat boarding area — no dogs nearby
  • Individual enclosures with cosy bedding and hiding spots
  • Quiet, calm environment with minimal noise and disruption
  • Climate-controlled rooms (essential for long-coated breeds in Kolkata's heat)
  • Daily coat grooming to prevent matting
  • Litter cleaned multiple times daily

Persian Cat Coat Care During Boarding

The Persian cat's signature long, flowing coat is beautiful but high-maintenance. Without daily brushing, Persian coats quickly develop mats and tangles that can cause skin irritation and pain. During Kolkata's humid monsoon season, coat care becomes even more critical.

Our trained cat care staff provides daily grooming for Persian cats including:

  • Gentle brushing with appropriate combs and slicker brushes
  • Eye area cleaning (Persians are prone to tear staining)
  • Ear inspection and cleaning
  • Mat detection and gentle removal
  • Full bathing and coat conditioning for extended stays

Feeding and Diet for Boarding Persian Cats

Persian cats have specific dietary considerations due to their flat face structure — they need food that is easy to pick up and chew. We accommodate all feeding preferences:

  • Premium Persian-specific kibble (flat-face friendly shape)
  • Wet food portions for hydration support
  • Fresh water available at all times (many Persians prefer running water fountains)
  • Hairball prevention supplements as needed

We follow your existing feeding schedule precisely to minimise digestive disruption and stress.

Health Monitoring for Persian Cats

Persian cats are predisposed to several health conditions that our staff monitors during boarding:

  • Respiratory issues: Flat-faced (brachycephalic) Persians can have breathing difficulties, especially in heat
  • Eye problems: Excessive tearing, discharge or redness is monitored daily
  • Kidney concerns: We monitor water intake and litter box habits
  • Coat and skin: Daily checks for fungal infections, especially during monsoon

Our 24/7 veterinary coordination ensures any health concern receives immediate professional attention.
