# Width 768 (tablet) - v2

Image: extra/width-768.png (all images loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: Neither a stretched phone nor a cramped desktop so much as an overlong single column with a tablet's worth of width doing nothing: the mid page stacks full width and one large orange circle dominates.

## Strengths:
- Color holds a point of view: burgundy ground, cream paper, amber as the single action colour, consistent from header to footer.
- Card interiors use a horizontal photo-left, copy-right arrangement in pricing and blog rows: a genuine tablet pattern, not a phone stack.
- Real photography throughout: dogs, handlers, a portrait, a service van.
- Section rhythm is legible: cream bands separated by full-bleed burgundy.

## Blocking:
- [Imagery] A large solid orange circle sits alone in the upper mid page with nothing inside it, surrounded by blank gutter. It reads as a failed image or unfinished section. Replace it with the intended photo, or delete the block and rebalance spacing.
- [Hierarchy] Roughly nine identical amber Book Now pills are spread down the page at the same weight. Pick one primary action per band, keep it amber, demote the rest to text links.
- [Responsiveness] Several sections terminate in a tall empty band, most visibly under the grooming card and above the subscribe strip. Set one vertical rhythm token for section padding at this breakpoint.

## Important:
- [Responsiveness] The services row keeps three narrow cards at 768, each a third of the width. Go 2-up or convert to three horizontal cards.
- [Responsiveness] The hero appears to be the phone composition centred in a 768 window, with wide empty flanks. Give tablet its own hero: headline and CTA left, image right at reduced height.
- [Typography] The heading scale does not step up between phone and tablet; the h1 keeps the same measure with dead space beside it.
- [Composition] The deepest burgundy appears at least four times, so the palette has no crescendo. Reserve the darkest band for the final CTA.
- [Responsiveness] The pricing trio is three full-width stacked cards, adding roughly three screens of scroll. Use a 2-up grid or a compact comparison table.
- [Accessibility] Accordion rows and footer text look small against burgundy and close in value; raise body copy to a 16px floor and footer to 14px, and check maroon-on-maroon.
- [States] No hover, focus or active state is visible, and no reduced-motion handling is evident.

## Polish:
- Equalise icon and label insets in the three trust cards.
- The footer collapses to one narrow stack with a large dead field; balance into two columns.
- The testimonial headshot is a plain rectangle while service photos use rounded crops; match.
- The add-on list is a stack of near-identical bars; give the price its own column.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Replace the empty orange circle with real content and remove the dead space around it.
