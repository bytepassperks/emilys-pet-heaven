# Mobile squint (390) - v2

Image: squint-mobile.png
Role: squint

Verdict: The value hierarchy is legible only as colour bands, not as a lead: dark chrome at both ends and a middle that is a flat field of equal-weight cream blocks.

## Strengths:
- The ground is committed: one warm cream field carries the whole scroll, so the strip reads as a single page.
- Chrome is consistent: the maroon header and footer bracket the page and match.
- Rhythm is even: no band is broken or colliding; spacing holds at this blur.

First three things, in order:
1. The dark maroon top bar.
2. The orange band directly beneath it.
3. The purple block further down.

Then the eye drops into a long run of cream cards and does not land until the dark footer.

## Blocking:
- [Hierarchy] No dominant mass. The darkest values are the header and footer, which are furniture; the hero and content sit at similar mid-light value. Give the hero one high-area dark or saturated mass, a full-bleed hero image or filled panel, that outweighs everything below.
- [Composition] The mobile view reads as a squeezed desktop column: near-equal-height cream cards stacked with the same gutter. Give it one full-bleed hero, then one wide card, then grouped rows at differing heights, and let one section break the column edge.

## Important:
- [Color] Orange and purple both read as saturated singletons at similar weight, with maroon a third. Keep one accent for actions and demote or remove the other.
- [Imagery] Mid-page image blocks dissolve into the cream ground, so the middle reads as empty. Crop tighter or set photos inside a contrasting surround.
- [Hierarchy] Repeated cream card rows have no internal value difference, so four or five read as one mass. Let one card in each group be filled or darker.
- [Motion] and [states] are not visible in a still; not inferred.

## Polish:
- The green sliver and tan block appear as isolated notes; confirm they belong to the role set.
- The gap above the footer is the one place the rhythm widens; make that consistent or tighten it.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Put one dark, high-area mass in the hero so the top visibly outweighs the uniform cream card field beneath it.
