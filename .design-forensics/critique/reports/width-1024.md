# Width 1024 (small laptop) - v2

Image: extra/width-1024.png (all images loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: The page holds at 1024 with no visible horizontal clipping and a steady cream and burgundy rhythm, but the first screen has no animal in it, one band imports a violet that belongs to no other section, and the location block ships as an empty panel.

## Strengths:
- No card, image or decorative shape is clipped or overflowing at 1024; three-column rows keep their gutters.
- Section rhythm is deliberate: cream and burgundy alternation gives the long page a pulse.
- Imagery is real: vet and groomer photography, pets in every service card, a phone mockup wrapping a rendered screen.
- The blog, testimonial and booking splits are asymmetric, so the page is not three-up rows throughout.

## Blocking:
- [Imagery] Find Us renders as a wide, near-blank pale panel above the footer. It reads as a map that failed to load. Embed a real map with a static fallback, and put address, hours and a directions link beside it.

## Important:
- [Accessibility] Primary buttons look like white or cream text on mid orange, and the hero feature labels are small caps at caption size. Switch button text to dark ink on orange or deepen the orange, and lift feature labels to at least 14px; measure both.
- [Responsiveness] The header packs logo, full nav, contact and CTA into one line at 1024 with nav type near body size. Below about 1120px, collapse two secondary links into a menu or More item and give the CTA a minimum width.
- [Color] The boarding band is violet; burgundy, orange and cream are the committed palette. Restate it on burgundy or cream, and reserve green for WhatsApp.
- [Composition] Three equal three-up rows plus tip cards give the default skeleton. Make one row asymmetric and give the pricing row a visually distinct middle plan.
- [Typography] The hero and section headings sit in a narrow size band, so the hero only mildly outranks the next heading. Push the hero up one step and drop section headings one step.
- [Imagery] The large orange disc in Welcome occupies close to a third of the row and carries no visible content at this width. Crop a pet photo into it, put a verified statistic inside, or remove it.

## Polish:
- Pricing feature lists are the densest text at 1024; raise line height before adding bullets.
- Floating chat buttons sit over the footer's right column; add bottom padding.
- No hover, focus or active state is visible, and testimonial dots are small targets; confirm focus rings and enlarge the dots.
- The overlapping cat avatars under the hero card read like a testimonial count but are unlabelled.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Put one real, large animal photograph into the first screen: move a strong pet photo into the hero as a full-height half or full-bleed band and let the white card sit on it.
