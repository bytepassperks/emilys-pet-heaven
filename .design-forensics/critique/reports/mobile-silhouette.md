# Mobile silhouette (390) - v2

Image: silhouette-mobile.png
Role: silhouette

Verdict: A stock mobile landing silhouette (dark header, image hero, two uniform card clusters, a long empty run, a striped ticker band, dark footer) that could belong to any groomer, vet, boarder or dog walker.

## Strengths:
- Single clean column; every block stays inside one width, so the page reads as one spine.
- Consistent dark/light alternation through the top half gives a steady cadence and clear section boundaries.
- One band breaks the pattern: the repeated thin dark stripes near the bottom are the only high-frequency texture.

## Blocking:
- [Composition] A long empty light run sits in the lower third, roughly a sixth of the page height, with no block. It reads as a dropped section. Replace it with one structurally different band: a full-bleed image with one line, or a split band.
- [Hierarchy] Three dark masses carry near-equal weight: hero image, card cluster, footer. Make the hero decisively largest and reduce the card cluster to lighter, smaller masses.

## Important:
- [Composition] The card cluster repeats two to three blocks at identical height and width with no featured item. Promote one card to full width so the cluster has a lead.
- [Composition] The sequence is the category default: dark nav, hero image, feature cards, testimonial, CTA, footer. Swap one band for something only this business could have, such as a before/after groom pair or a booking band with real time slots.
- [Responsiveness] The uniform stack reads as a reflowed desktop grid. Give mobile its own rhythm: one oversized card, then a horizontally scrolling strip.
- [Color] The striped band is the only value-contrast spike, so it pulls harder than the CTA above it. Promote it into the action band or remove the stripes.

## Polish:
- The dark header and footer bookend the page consistently; keep that pairing.
- The near-square hero mass could crop to portrait, increasing its lead without adding height.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Delete the empty run and rebuild that slot as the page's one distinctive band: a full-bleed pet image paired with the booking action.
