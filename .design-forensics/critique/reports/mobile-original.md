# Mobile original (390) - v2

Image: mobile.png (all images loaded; hero CTAs clear the pet strip)
Role: original

Verdict: The palette gives the page a real point of view, but the structure is the standard local-service template and amber does so many jobs that nothing leads.

## Strengths:
- Color has a committed direction: oxblood ground, cream page, amber action, consistent across hero, bands and footer. Not the pastel-teal pet default.
- Imagery is real throughout: dogs, cats and a person, with a device frame wrapping a rendered screen.
- The mobile view has its own composition, full-width stacked blocks and single-column rows, not a squeezed desktop grid.
- Content is specific: Barrackpore named in the hero and repeated in a find-us section, prices in rupees, a named trainer.

## Blocking:
- [Hierarchy] Three full-bleed CTA bands plus an amber pill on almost every card, and the booking section is a wall of four or five identical amber buttons. Collapse the booking wall into one primary button plus a compact three-row service chooser with one marked most booked.
- [Composition] The page repeats the same unit (photo, heading, two lines, amber CTA) roughly eight times across sixteen sections, with maroon bands as the only rhythm change. Cut to six sections and give the three maroon bands distinct jobs: promise, proof, action.

## Important:
- [Color] Amber is the headline accent, every button, the pricing card grounds, several band grounds and the icon colour. Reserve amber for primary buttons; make pricing cards cream with one amber recommended tier.
- [Hierarchy] The hero carries wordmark, headline, subcopy, two buttons, the pet strip, then an amber announcement band. Drop the announcement below the hero, cut the subcopy to one line, and let the headline own the first screen.
- [Accessibility] Small body copy in maroon bands and cream cards reads muted and thin; measure and lift the muted body colour until every pair passes.
- [Accessibility] Footer links and social icons are small inline strings. Make footer links block-level rows at 44px minimum and give social icons a proper hit area.
- [Imagery] Photo treatment is inconsistent: full-bleed rounded cards, small thumbnails, an overlapping collage, one promo graphic with a drawn red arrow. One crop ratio and one radius; delete the arrow.
- [Composition] The find-us block reads as a flat pale panel. Confirm a real embedded map with a pin renders, and add a tappable Get directions action.

## Polish:
- Give one pricing tier a badge so the three cards are not visually equal.
- The pet strip is decorative: give it a job or remove it.
- Collapse footer columns into an accordion on mobile.
- Verify heading order separately.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Make amber mean one thing: primary buttons only, and collapse the booking wall to one primary action plus a three-row service chooser.
