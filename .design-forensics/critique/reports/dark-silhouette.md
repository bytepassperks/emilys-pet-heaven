# Dark silhouette (1280) - v2

Image: silhouette-dark.png
Role: silhouette

Verdict: A competent but interchangeable local-service skeleton (split hero, two zig-zag rows, then two equal card grids), legible as blocks but not as detail.

## Strengths:
- Composition: the hero holds the single largest light mass on the page, so the top has one clear anchor.
- Composition: the alternating light/dark split rows give a left-right pulse through the upper middle.
- Hierarchy: the dark three-card band and the dark footer bracket the lower page, giving visible weight top and bottom.

## Blocking:
- [Composition] The lower half resolves into two consecutive equal-weight card grids, a four-up and a three-up, with near-identical mass. Collapse into one asymmetric services composition with one offering at roughly double the block size, and give the remaining section a different shape.
- [Hierarchy] After the hero there is no second scale peak. Every block sits inside a narrow size band. Choose one middle section to carry a decisive scale jump and tighten the gaps around it.

## Important:
- [Composition] The two split rows repeat the identical light/dark alternation, so the second contributes no new information. Keep one and convert the other into an offset overlapping composition or a horizontal strip of three narrow crops.
- [Composition] A full-width horizontal band between the four-up grid and the dark three-up reads as a seam. Merge it into the section above or make it an unmistakable full-bleed quote.
- [Composition] The footer containment breaks: a thin full-width light strip sits below the dark footer block. Fold it into the footer or remove it.
- [Hierarchy] The circle portrait is the only round shape and sits alone in a wide, low-contrast row, reading as an accident. Commit to the circle as a motif or pull it into a two-column composition.

## Polish:
- Vertical gaps are uniform end to end; introduce two distinct gap sizes so pacing is visible.
- Only two large photographic masses exist; one more mid-page would balance the light and dark distribution.
- Nothing in the skeleton says pet care: no before/after block, no gallery rhythm, no booking emphasis.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Replace the two consecutive equal card grids with one asymmetric services composition where a single offering leads at roughly double scale, then give the remaining section a genuinely different block shape.
