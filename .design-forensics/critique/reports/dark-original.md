# Dark original (1280) - v2

Image: dark.png (all images loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: The maroon sections are a committed dark brand surface, but roughly half the page is still untouched cream, so dark reads as maroon-with-light-bands rather than one intentional composition, and two bands look broken.

## Strengths:
- The deep maroon ground is a real brand decision, not a neutral near-black. Cream on maroon holds up and looks deliberate.
- The hero does one specific thing for this product: a row of circular pet photos under the headline.
- The pricing band is the most coherent stretch: dark ground, cream heading, three cream cards with dark type.
- Section rhythm is legible from colour alone.

## Blocking:
- [Imagery] Find Us is an empty white rectangle where the map should be; in dark it is the brightest object and reads as a failed embed. Render the real map or a static map styled for the ground.
- [Color] The Welcome band is dominated by a large solid orange disc with nothing in it. It reads as a missing image and is the first thing the eye hits after the hero. Put a real photo inside it or drop the disc.
- [Accessibility] Pricing feature lists and footer links look washed out light-on-cream and dim-on-maroon; measure and promote them to the text token.

## Important:
- [Color] Cream bands are full-bleed and full brightness, so a dark session flashes between every maroon section. Give dark its own raised surface or stop calling it dark mode.
- [Hierarchy] Five consecutive rows of three equal cards: hero support, services, trust badges, pricing, blog. Break at least two into asymmetric layouts.
- [Typography] Every section heading is centred and near the same size; no scale contrast. Give the hero and section openers one display size and step the rest down.
- [Composition] The sequence is the default local-service template. Only the pet photo row is shaped by pet boarding.
- [Hierarchy] The final CTA puts filled orange and filled green side by side at equal weight, and the hero does the same. Keep one filled primary and make the second a ghost.
- [Color] Green appears as one pill, one check and one button with no fixed role. Commit it to availability or success, or remove it.

## Polish:
- The trust strip is three thin cards of tiny type; one horizontal strip would say the same more quietly.
- The circular Meet badge overlaps the emergency photo with no visible job.
- The cream logo tile in the footer is brighter than the link text and pulls the eye to the least important element.
- Top utility bar text is nearly invisible against maroon.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Give dark its own surface system: replace full-bleed cream bands with raised dark panels carrying cream type, keeping cream only for pricing cards, and fix the empty map in the same pass.
