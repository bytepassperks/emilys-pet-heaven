# Dark squint (1280) - v2

Image: squint-dark.png
Role: squint

Verdict: A committed maroon and cream palette with one orange accent, but at squint no single element leads: five bands of near-equal weight stack down the page and orange appears three times.

## Strengths:
- Color: the palette is genuinely committed: maroon ground, cream panels, one orange accent.
- Composition: the bands run full-bleed and share one column width, so the page reads as one object.
- The three-panel trio mid-page has even spacing and consistent gutters, which holds even blurred.

## Blocking:
- [Hierarchy] Nothing leads. Header maroon, orange cluster, cream band, maroon band with three cream cards, white blocks, cream, maroon footer: the masses are close enough in area and contrast that attention spreads evenly. Give one hero element a size and value step nothing else repeats.
- [Color] Orange is the highest chroma and appears at least three times: a large filled circle, two rectangular blocks near the top, a button near the footer. Reserve orange for exactly one job.

## Important:
- [Composition] The sequence is the default stack: header, hero, three equal cards, alternating panels, footer. Break the trio into an asymmetric arrangement or give one section a full-bleed image.
- [Imagery] Photographic blocks in the lower half are small and inside cream panels, so they read as texture. One real photo at hero scale, edge to edge.
- [Hierarchy] White and cream bands alternate at similar heights, producing stripes. Make bands unequal: one tall quiet band and one short dense band.

## Polish:
- The footer maroon matches the header, which bookends correctly, but nothing between carries that value, so the page ends without a visual stop.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Give the top maroon band one dominant cream headline at hero scale and cut orange down to a single button, so the squint resolves to headline first and everything else second.
