# Width 1920 (full desktop) - v2

Image: extra/width-1920.png (all images loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: At 1920 the page survives on palette and photography but does not hold its width: the hero, subscribe band and closing CTA sit as small centred islands inside wide colour fields.

## Strengths:
- Color has a real point of view: maroon ground, cream surface, amber accent, applied consistently from header to footer.
- The pricing section is content-specific: small, medium and large breeds is what a grooming business actually sells.
- Real photography throughout; groomer, vet and dog images give the page warmth.
- Brand surface stays consistent across the full scroll.

## Blocking:
- [Hierarchy / Responsiveness] The hero is a centred text column in the middle third of 1920 on a flat maroon field, with a small subline and two centred buttons. The sides are dead colour and the hero reads as stranded. Build a two-column hero above 1200px: headline and CTAs left-aligned at display size, one strong real photograph filling the right half.
- [Imagery] Find Us is an empty white rectangle under its heading. A local service business with an empty location block breaks trust. Use a static map plus pin, address and hours, or drop the section.

## Important:
- [Color] Amber is both action colour and large surface fill, so it no longer signals click. White labels on amber and amber eyebrows on cream read washed. Darken the fill or use maroon labels, and measure.
- [Composition] The subscribe band and the closing CTA are the same small centred island as the hero, roughly 400 to 500px of content in a 1920 field. Collapse them into one distinct conversion block.
- [Composition] The stack is the default long landing sequence; the three-card row appears twice and every section is eyebrow, heading, subline. Break it once: make Welcome full-bleed image against text and vary a card count.
- [Imagery] The hero pet strip is cutout animals at visibly different scales with a ragged bottom edge; it reads as a sticker sheet. Normalise to one baseline and scale, or remove it once the hero has a real photograph.
- [Typography] One rounded geometric sans carries display and body with no pairing and no decisive scale jump. Set a display face with character and keep a neutral text face.
- [States] A cluster of floating widgets (yellow pill, green WhatsApp, orange chat) stacks on the right edge and overlaps card edges mid-page. Confirm hover and focus states on nav, buttons and fields.

## Polish:
- Header nav and phone are tiny relative to 1920; increase header height and nav size slightly.
- Heading alignment is mixed; commit to one spine.
- The two dog photos in Welcome have mismatched heights; align baselines.
- Two blog cards across a wide column are sparse; use three-up or one featured plus a list.
- Footer link rows are tight and low contrast; loosen line height and check the link colour.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Rebuild the hero as a two-column grid above 1200px: display-scale headline and CTAs left-aligned, one real photograph filling the right half, pet strip removed or demoted to a thin band.
