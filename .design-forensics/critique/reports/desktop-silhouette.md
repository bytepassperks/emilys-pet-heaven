# Desktop silhouette (1280) - v2

Image: silhouette-desktop.png
Role: silhouette

Verdict: The generic pet-shop template stack: hero, feature trio, gallery strip, three cards, quote, two-up, CTA, footer, with exactly one asymmetric block that suggests a point of view and is never repeated.

## Strengths:
- Three dark anchors (upper band, mid band, footer) break the page into chapters.
- One wide band puts an organic off-centre mass at the right edge against left text; that asymmetry is the only structurally specific gesture.
- The outlined three-column card row reads as a deliberate unit, not three floating boxes.

## Blocking:
- [Composition] The section order is the category default: hero, three small blocks, wide feature band, five-up gallery, three cards, quote, two-up, CTA, footer. Cut one band and replace it with something only a pet-heaven store would do, such as a find-your-pet strip or a full-bleed animal portrait with type.
- [Hierarchy] No mid-page block leads. Hero, feature band, card row and two-up are near-equal in height. Make one mid section roughly twice the height of its neighbours.

## Important:
- [Composition] The circle-plus-text pattern appears twice (mid and lower page). Merge into one testimonial moment.
- [Composition] A wide light band sits almost empty with one short line. Give it content or remove it.
- [Imagery] Only one block is non-rectangular, so the distinctive gesture looks accidental. Repeat the off-centre organic crop once.

## Polish:
- The five-up gallery is the densest row; widen it into a true full-bleed band.
- The footer's wide bar plus centred lines adds nothing; a left-aligned footer would echo the asymmetric mid band.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Delete one duplicated circle-plus-text band and one near-empty band, then give the freed space to the asymmetric image-plus-text section so the page has one unmistakable structural signature.
