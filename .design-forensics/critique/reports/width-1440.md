# Width 1440 (large laptop) - v2

Image: extra/width-1440.png (all images loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: The container holds at 1440, content stays centred with readable measure and does not stretch edge to edge, but the page is a flat march of equal-weight sections and repeated three-card rows, and the empty map box breaks trust before the footer.

## Strengths:
- Hierarchy at the top works: the hero headline is the largest element, one yellow primary CTA follows it, and the same yellow repeats in the nav.
- Color is a committed system: deep maroon ground, warm cream, a single yellow accent reserved for actions.
- Container discipline: side margins are consistent at 1440 and body copy keeps a sane measure.
- Vertical rhythm: even section padding with alternating cream, maroon and white bands cleanly separates topics.

## Blocking:
- [Imagery] Find Us is a large empty grey rectangle where a map should be. A visitor reads it as broken right before the footer and the booking decision. Embed a styled map or a static map image with a pin, or replace the block with a real address card and directions link.

## Important:
- [Hierarchy] Every section heading after the hero sits at nearly the same size and weight, so the long page reads as a list of equals. Make one or two sections visibly louder and let the rest step down.
- [Composition] Three equal-card rows repeat three times (services, pricing, blog). Break at least one into an asymmetric layout or a horizontal pricing comparison.
- [Composition] At 1440 the top utility trio and pricing columns are wide with thin content, leaving large empty horizontal space inside each card. Cap inner width or add real content so the width is earned.
- [Composition] Heading alignment alternates between centred and left with no clear rule. Pick one logic and hold it.
- [Imagery] The hero uses cartoon vector pets while every other section uses photography. Commit to one visual language.
- [Typography] The display face and section headings do not clearly differ in character; headings read as the same family bolded. Pair a distinct display with a neutral sans and widen the scale gap.

## Polish:
- Balance internal padding so cream and maroon bands breathe equally.
- The floating chat buttons overlap content at some scroll positions; anchor them clear of CTA buttons.
- Confirm body line length stays under about 75 characters in Welcome and testimonial.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Give one mid-page section a real scale jump, a full-bleed larger moment for services or pricing, and let the surrounding sections step down.
