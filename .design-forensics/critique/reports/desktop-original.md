# Desktop original (1280) - v2

Image: desktop.png (all images and fonts loaded)
Role: original

Verdict: A committed maroon-and-gold ground and a real hero photo strip, undermined by a timid headline, four competing action colours, and two dead-looking blocks that make the page read as unfinished.

## Strengths:
- Color: the maroon ground is a real decision and carries from hero through CTA and footer, giving the page a drumbeat.
- Imagery: the hero pet strip is real photography and names the category in under a second.
- Composition: the blog block (three small cards plus one large lead image) is the one place the layout departs from the default grid.
- The warm gold-on-maroon pairing suits a boarding and grooming business; caring rather than clinical.

## Blocking:
- [Imagery] The Welcome block contains a large flat orange disc where a photograph belongs, and Find Us ends in a pale rectangle where the map should render. Both read as broken. Replace the disc with a staff or facility photo, and embed a working map or show address plus hours.
- [Color] Four action colours: gold in the hero, orange mid-page, olive-green in the newsletter, dark in the final CTA. The eye learns no button signal and olive reads disabled. Use one accent for primary actions, one outline for secondary, delete olive.
- [Hierarchy] The hero headline is close to section-heading weight, so the largest element is the maroon field, not the message. Set the headline at display scale (60 to 72px desktop, tight leading, two to three lines) and step section headings down.

## Important:
- [Composition] Sequence is hero, three service cards, three pricing cards, testimonial, CTA, blog, newsletter, footer: the category default with nothing specific to pet boarding. Add facility photos, a before/after groom, or a handover policy.
- [Typography] One geometric sans does everything; hero and card titles have no contrast in voice. Pair a warm display face with a restrained text face.
- [Typography] "Pet Emergencies What You Need To" is grammatically incomplete. Complete it.
- [Color] The magenta promo card and blue-tinted service cards sit outside the maroon and gold system. Restate them in cream or maroon.
- [Accessibility] White on orange and olive looks below AA and card body text looks under 14px; measure. Use maroon text on gold and raise small text to 16px.
- [Composition] Floating contact icons stack at inconsistent sizes and overlap the content column mid-page. One vertical stack, 44px targets, clear of text.

## Polish:
- Hero badge cards repeat what nav and footer already say; keep one.
- All three pricing tiers look equally promoted; mark one recommended or none.
- Let the pet strip bleed edge to edge and overlap the hero base so it feels composed.
- Join the newsletter input and button into one control.
- Tighten the gap between the emergency block and pricing.
- Footer labels are small and low contrast against maroon.
- Motion and states cannot be judged from a still.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Rebuild the hero so the headline is unambiguously the largest element at display scale, with the pet strip as a full-bleed band beneath it and the two badge cards removed.
