# Desktop squint (1280) - v2

Image: squint-desktop.png
Role: squint

Verdict: The page reads as a vertical stripe pattern of alternating maroon and cream bands, and the eye lands on the dark bands rather than on any single message.

## Strengths:
- Color: two committed grounds, deep maroon and warm cream, with one orange accent used consistently. A palette with a point of view.
- Composition: the sequence is specific to a local pet service (photo of a person, portrait block, service tiles, testimonial rows), not a pure SaaS skeleton.
- Imagery: real photographic blocks mid and lower page, not grey placeholders.

## Blocking:
- [Hierarchy] The dark maroon bands carry more mass than the hero headline, so the eye goes to the mid-page card band and the three-card band before the top message. Nothing leads. Make the hero the single heaviest element and reduce later maroon bands.
- [Hierarchy] At least four maroon bands of near-identical value alternate with cream. They read as equal-weight stripes. Assign three distinct tones and use each once per screen.

## Important:
- [Composition] The maroon band with three equal cream cards is the default three-card row and the most prominent block after the hero. Replace it with one large card plus two stacked, or a horizontal service strip.
- [Color] Orange appears as a small circle, small tiles and buttons at similar sizes, so it reads as decoration. Reserve orange for actions only.
- [Hierarchy] The CTA is a small button inside a large maroon band near the bottom, similar to the header button. Make the primary action the largest solid orange element at the end of the hero and in the closing band.

## Polish:
- The dark footer and the dark closing band merge; separate them with a light divider or a distinct footer tone.
- The lower photo block is the same width and value as the text beside it; give it more area.

## Single highest-leverage change:
Collapse the page to one dark mass: hero only, largest type on the page, then lighten every later band so the squint shows a clear top-heavy shape instead of repeating stripes.
