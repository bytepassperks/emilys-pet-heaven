Essential Pet Care Tips for Barrackpore Pet Owners

Living in the Barrackpore and North 24 Parganas area comes with specific considerations for pet care:

  • Monsoon precautions: During the Bengal monsoon season (June-September), keep your pet's paws dry, watch for waterborne infections and ensure your home is tick and flea treated.
  • Summer heat care: Kolkata summers can be intense. Ensure fresh water is always available, avoid walks during peak heat (11 AM - 4 PM) and watch for signs of heatstroke.
  • Vaccination schedule: Keep your pet's vaccinations up to date, especially rabies, distemper and parvovirus. Local government veterinary hospitals offer affordable vaccination drives.
  • Stray dog awareness: When walking your dog, be cautious around stray dog territories. Keep your pet leashed and supervise interactions carefully.
  • Emergency contacts: Save the number of your nearest vet clinic and Emily's Pet Heaven (+91 6363590332) for pet emergencies.
