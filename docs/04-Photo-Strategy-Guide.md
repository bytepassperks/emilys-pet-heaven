## Geo-Tagging Instructions

**Every photo MUST be geo-tagged before uploading to GBP.**

### Method 1: Phone GPS (Easiest)
1. Take photos at the facility using your phone
2. Ensure Location Services / GPS is ON in Camera settings
3. Photos will automatically embed coordinates (22.7606°N, 88.3700°E)

### Method 2: Manual Geo-Tag (For existing photos)
1. Download "GeoTag Photos Pro" (iOS) or "Photo Map" (Android)
2. Set coordinates: **Latitude: 22.7606, Longitude: 88.3700**
3. Apply to all photos before uploading

### Method 3: Desktop Tool
1. Use ExifTool (free): `exiftool -GPSLatitude=22.7606 -GPSLongitude=88.3700 -GPSLatitudeRef=N -GPSLongitudeRef=E photo.jpg`
2. Apply to batch of photos at once

## Photo Quality Guidelines

1. **Resolution:** Minimum 720px wide. Ideal: 1200-2000px on longest side
2. **Format:** JPEG preferred. PNG acceptable. Avoid HEIC.
3. **File size:** Under 5MB per photo. Compress with TinyJPG if needed
4. **Lighting:** Natural light preferred. Avoid harsh flash
5. **Composition:** Clean backgrounds, no clutter. Focus on the subject
6. **Faces:** Get written permission before uploading photos with customer faces
7. **Branding:** Include subtle branding (logo on wall, branded t-shirt) where possible
8. **Freshness:** Add 2-3 new photos every week after initial upload. Google rewards fresh content
9. **No stock photos:** Every photo must be of YOUR actual facility and real pets
10. **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 or 16:9 landscape works best in GBP listings
