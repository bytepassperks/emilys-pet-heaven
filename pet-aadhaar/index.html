Pet Aadhaar Card — FAQ

What is a Pet Aadhaar Card?

A personalised novelty pet ID card showing your pet's name, owner, breed, gender, DOB and address, with a unique Pet ID number and a scannable QR code that opens the pet's verification page on emilyspetheaven.com.

How much does it cost?

₹199 for one card and ₹349 for two. Shipping is extra and every card is delivered laminated.

How do I order?

Fill the form above to see a live preview, then tap “Order on WhatsApp”, send your pet's photo and pay. We generate the unique Pet ID + QR, share a soft copy and ship the laminated card.

Does the QR code actually work?

Yes — scanning it with any phone camera opens that pet's verification page on our website, useful for authenticity and for returning a lost pet.

Is this an official government document?

No. It is a fun, personalised novelty keepsake and pet-security tag by Emily's Pet Heaven, not a government-issued document.
